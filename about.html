MZ News & Article brings together stories across Tamil, English, Hindi, Telugu and Malayalam. Our aim is to make important developments easier to find and understand, wherever you read.

What we cover

Explore updates across politics, business, technology, science, world affairs, sports, health and culture. Our pages are organized by topic and language so readers can follow the subjects that matter to them.

Designed for multilingual readers

Language sections make it easier to browse news in five languages. Each language edition can reflect its readers while following the same standards for accuracy, attribution and corrections.
