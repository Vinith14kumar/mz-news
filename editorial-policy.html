Editorial Policy

The standards we use to prepare, present and correct editorial content.

Accuracy, fairness and clear attribution are the foundation of useful reporting. These standards apply to stories published across our language editions.

Verification and sourcing

We aim to check important claims against primary documents, direct statements or multiple credible sources. Reports that remain uncertain should be described with appropriate context rather than presented as established fact.

Fairness and context

Coverage should represent relevant perspectives, distinguish fact from opinion and avoid headlines that overstate what the underlying material supports. Images and quotations should not be used in a misleading context.

Corrections and updates

When a material error is identified, the story should be corrected promptly and the change made clear to readers. Updates that add context without correcting an error should be identified as updates where appropriate.

Language editions

Translations should preserve the meaning and context of the original report. Editors should not add unsupported claims or remove essential qualifications when adapting a story for another language.

Advertising and conflicts

Paid or sponsored content must be clearly labelled. Advertisers do not approve or direct editorial reporting, and potential conflicts should be disclosed and managed.

This policy describes the intended standards for this publication. The current site is a static demonstration and its sample stories may not reflect a live editorial workflow.
