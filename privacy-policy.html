This page describes the current static demonstration build. Its login and signup screens do not connect to an account service, and submitted credentials are not sent to a server by this project.

Saved articles

The Save for later feature stores saved story titles and links in your browser's local storage under the key mzNewsSavedArticles. This data remains on that browser until you remove saved stories or clear the browser's site data. It is not synchronized to another device or sent to an MZ News server.

Forms and account screens

Login and signup pages currently provide front-end demonstration flows only. Do not enter a real password or sensitive personal information. The Contact page's Copy message control copies text to your clipboard; it does not submit that message.

Search and external resources

News searches may appear in the page URL as a query parameter. Pages also request resources from third-party hosts, including Google Fonts, Unsplash and Wikimedia Commons. Those providers may receive standard connection information, such as your IP address and browser details, when the resource is requested; their own privacy policies apply.

Analytics and cookies

This project contains no analytics integration or advertising cookie system in its current build. If those services or a backend are added, this policy should be updated to describe the data collected and the choices available to readers.

Your choices

You can remove saved articles from the Saved tab or clear this site's local storage in your browser settings. You can also block third-party resources through browser privacy controls, though some images or fonts may then be unavailable.

This policy is informational copy for the current demo, not legal advice. Review it with qualified counsel before using the site to collect personal data.
