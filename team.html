MZ News & Article is organized around the work behind a reliable reading experience. This page describes the editorial functions that support the publication; individual staff profiles will be added when confirmed.

How the newsroom works

Editorial desk

Reviews story selection, context, sourcing and corrections before publication.

Language editors

Prepare language editions with attention to meaning, local context and clarity.

Research and verification

Checks claims against available documents, primary sources and credible reporting.

Product and audience

Maintains the reading experience across devices, topics and language sections.

Editorial independence

Commercial relationships do not determine editorial decisions. Sponsored material, when introduced, must be clearly identified and kept separate from independent coverage.
